About Us:

We are a small, student-led, business from Ormiston Junior College. Unique Cookies is a non-profit business, where we donate all the money to The Cookie Project. This charity is uses the donations and pays minimum wage for people with disabilities. This is our way of giving back to our community to help others as they did to us. By ordering a cookie from us, not only do you receive a beautiful, hand-crafted cookie, but this is how even you can give back to the community.
Most other cookie companies, are expensive and do not offer a diverse selection. We try our hardest to craft the most high quality cookies while keeping the prices as low as possible, including discounts if your order is over $15. We also offer a wide range of delicious from being able to customise the cookie base, frosting flavours, and type of topping, allowing you to create your own Unique Cookie.
